# Changelog

## Unreleased

Round 5: the editorial review, a second domain, and what does not fit. `cases/editorial-review/` holds a schema (an article, an author, two reviewers who must both approve a version, an editor who publishes by a final goal, a deadline written as a line) and three scenarios (`published`, `late`, `left-alone`); `cases/scenario.ts` is the one resolver of scenarios, with the placeholders `$line`, `$goal` and `$find`, `playScenario`, and steps that may be `confirmed` or expected `refused`, and the tests and the playground both import it. SIBAR's two examples moved into the same shape (`cases/sibar-activity/scenarios.json`), and its policy into `policy.json`. The engine's suite plays the three scenarios, with assertions at the steps that matter (`editorial.test.ts`), the resolver and SIBAR's two (`scenarios.test.ts`), and pins one test to each of five misfits, with a `collect-writer` rule kind registered in a kit inside the test, which removes the `stamp` the schema needed; [docs/misfits.md](docs/misfits.md) records the five, each with what the engine says today and the smallest change that would make it fit (a check that reads the current value, a check on a value and a negated one, alternatives in `when.values`, a quorum on a goal, a base kind that reads the writer). Nothing in the engine changed. The playground has a domain picker, «Dominio»: each domain is a module in `playground/src/domains/` holding its schema, UI schema, people, policy and examples, switching resets the thread, and no gist is named anywhere else (`playground/test/fence.test.ts`; `domains.test.ts` plays every example through the engine). 340 tests in `consequitur` (from 312), 48 in `consequitur-render`, 18 in the playground.

Round 4, answers: a failed preview reaches the application, and the laws are a chip. `previewer` takes `onError(error, draft)`, called when the run of the draft now on screen rejects; the answer on screen stays, not fresh, so nothing is confirmed on it (swallowing the failure was ruled out). `band` gains a read-only `dragging`, so an application keeps no flag of its own; the playground's `pointing.ts` uses it and keeps only the `is-linking` class. The playground says a failed preview in the draft's preview block («Anteprima non disponibile: …») and «Schemi» has «Leggi» among its chips, showing the schema's `laws` as code, besides «Testa» and one chip per gist. 48 tests in `consequitur-render`, from 41.

Round 4, second commit: the playground. `playground/` is a private workspace package, a sample application on the SIBAR case: the thread bench of `sibardoc-prototype` ported onto `consequitur` and `consequitur-render`, with plain TypeScript and DOM, `markdown-it` and Vite (port 5588). It reads `cases/sibar-activity/schema.json` with `readSchema` and `BASE_KIT`, passes the SIBAR policy (quits on a closed activity are not answered) to `post` and `dryRun`, and draws the ledger with `keyed`, the preview with `previewer`, the pointing with `band` and each card's reading with `lineFacts`. It has the composer with `@` and `#` tokens, the dry-run preview with «Conferma e invia», the goal dock with two goals and the completion wrapper, «Cambia» as revoke and new, both examples, and the inspector with the schema as code («Testa», each gist, the laws). `pnpm dev` and `pnpm build` at the root run it; `pnpm typecheck` covers `playground/src`. The spec is [specs/playground.md](docs/specs/playground.md).

Round 4, first commit: `consequitur-render`. `lineFacts` reads each line of a thread (in force, revoked by, goal, written by a goal, warnings, locked); `keyed` keeps a node per line id and rebuilds one only when its key changes; `previewer` runs the dry run after a quiet delay and drops stale answers, so an application confirms only what it saw; `band` draws the pointer line over a layer, for a drag from a slot to a line and for a hover. No markup, no style, no runtime dependency but `consequitur`. 41 tests, `jsdom` for the three that touch the DOM. The spec is [specs/render.md](docs/specs/render.md).

Round 3: kinds in a kit, a schema that names its own words, the policy and the diff. Handle kinds (`value`, `set`), rule kinds (`value`, `join`, `leave`) and law kinds (`keeps-set`, `keeps-goals`, `settles`) are registered in a `Kit`, `BASE_KIT` holds the base ones, and a schema names the kinds it uses; revoke and goal stay mechanics. `readSchema(json, kit)` reads the whole schema: its head (`opening`, `authority`, `sets`, `records`, `judged`), its gists and its laws, and never throws. Handles are `{ value, set }`, denunciations of a handle are `sort: 'handle'`, a records' payload is `{ set, member, line? }`, `when` reads `in` and `values`, and the opening rule is `one-opening`. New: `Policy` and `answer`, `diff`, `emptyState`, and `offers` computing the expectations once per call. SIBAR's gists and laws moved out of the package into `cases/sibar-activity/schema.json`, which the tests read; `BASE_GIST_DATA`, `BASE_GISTS`, `BASE_LAW_DATA`, `BASE_LAWS`, `CREATE_GIST`, `WARNING_GIST` and `SYSTEM_WRITER` are gone. The 250 tests keep their meaning in the new words, and 62 more cover what is new: 312 in all. [architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) is written.

Round 2: the monorepo, and the engine ported as it is. A pnpm workspace with two packages. `consequitur` holds the engine copied from `sibardoc-prototype` (`packages/core/src/thread/`) and its 250 tests, with no change of meaning: the only edits are `.ts` extensions on the relative imports, the test's import path, and the folder-fence test, which now reads `src/` and expects the extensions. `consequitur-render` is empty until round 4. Both are at 0.0.0 and export their sources directly; publishing is round 6.
