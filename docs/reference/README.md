# Reference

The machinery as it is, one page per area, in the order a reader meets them.

- [The thread](thread.md): the line, its envelope and id, the declaration, the reading order.
- [The schema and the kit](schema.md): `readSchema`, the head, gist schemas, rule and law bindings, the kit and the interfaces of its kinds.
- [Conditions](conditions.md): the condition language, its references, its three-valued answer, named guards, checks and the trace.
- [The reading](reading.md): `consequitur`, in force and revoked, expectations, goals, holes and denunciations.
- [Posting](posting.md): `admit`, `dryRun`, `post`, `settle`, laws and verdicts, the policy, `diff`, and the refusal codes.
- [Offers](offers.md): `offers`, `whenHolds` and `holds`.
- [Scenarios](scenarios.md): `playScenario`, placeholders, `prune`, `stepsOf`, `holed` and `replayed`.
- [Consolidation](consolidation.md): `consolidate`, `deriveSchema` and its conflicts, the seals, the drill.
- [consequitur-render](render.md): `lineFacts`, `changes`, `keyed`, `previewer` and `band`.
- [consequitur-kdl](kdl.md): `fromKdl`, `toKdl`, and the KDL form of a schema.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): every name the project uses, and what it means.
